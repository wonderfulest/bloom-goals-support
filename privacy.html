Privacy Policy

Bloom Goals Privacy Policy
Effective October 20, 2026

1. Local use, no account
Bloom Goals records personal goals and progress. There is no sign-up or login. Goal titles, targets, units, plants, entry dates and notes are saved only in the app’s storage on your device.

2. We do not collect or upload your data
This version does not send goals or usage records to the developer. It has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and does not call AI services at runtime. The plant illustrations are prepared in advance and bundled with the app.

3. Storage and deletion
Data remains until you delete a goal or remove app data. Open a goal and use its top-right menu to delete the goal and all its entries, or undo an individual entry. The app has no recovery feature after deletion. Uninstalling the app may erase local data.

4. System backups
The app has no separate cloud sync service. iOS or your computer may back up app data according to your system settings. Those backups are managed through your settings with Apple and do not give the developer access to your data. Deleting a local goal does not delete existing system backups.

5. Permissions and sharing
This version does not request camera, photos, location, contacts or health data permissions. We do not sell, share or provide your goal records to third parties.

6. Contact and websites
App publisher: wonderfulest. For privacy questions, visit the support website in Settings. GitHub feedback is public, so please do not include private goals or sensitive information. The support and privacy websites are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit a website or submit feedback, GitHub handles network requests and account information under its own privacy statement. This is separate from the app’s offline data storage.

7. Policy updates
If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and provide any necessary explanations and choices. This version works entirely offline.

隐私政策

慢慢开花隐私政策
生效日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日

1. 本地使用，无需账号
慢慢开花用于记录个人目标及进度，不提供注册或登录。目标名称、总量、单位、花种、记录日期和备注仅由应用保存于当前设备的应用空间。

2. 我们不收集或上传数据
本版本不将你的目标或使用记录发送给开发者，不包含广告、行为分析、追踪或第三方 SDK，也不在使用时调用 AI 服务。应用中的植物插画是预先制作并随应用打包的静态素材。

3. 保存和删除
数据保留到你删除目标或移除应用数据为止。你可以打开目标详情，通过右上角菜单删除目标及其全部成长记录，或撤销单条记录。删除后本应用不提供恢复功能。卸载应用可能导致本地数据丢失。

4. 系统备份
本应用不提供独立云同步服务。iOS 或你的电脑可能按你的系统设置备份应用数据；这些备份由你与 Apple 的相应设置管理，不向本应用开发者提供数据访问权限。删除本机目标不代表同时删除已有系统备份。

5. 权限和共享
本版本无需相机、照片、位置、通讯录或健康数据权限。我们不出售、共享或向第三方提供你的目标记录。

6. 政策更新
如果后续版本改变数据处理方式，我们会更新本政策，并在需要时提供相应说明与选择。当前版本可完全离线使用。
