Bloom Goals — Support

Grow a small goal into a hand-painted flower, at your own pace. Available in English and Simplified Chinese.

Contact support

Report a problem or share a suggestion · View existing feedback

GitHub feedback is public and requires a GitHub account; the app itself does not. Please include only your issue, device model and iOS version. Do not include private goals or sensitive information.

How does a flower bloom?

Tap Plant a Goal and choose a target and unit. Add Progress as you work toward it. When you reach the target, the flower joins My Garden. No real-world waiting period is required.

Can I take a break?

Yes. Your progress stays with you. Return whenever you are ready.

How do I rename, delete or correct a goal?

Open the goal, then use Manage Goal in the top-right corner. Deleting a goal removes all its entries and flower and cannot be undone. Use the undo button beside an entry to correct a mistake.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account or separate cloud sync. Uninstalling may erase local data; system backups depend on your device settings.

Language and region

Chinese language settings use Simplified Chinese. Other languages default to English. Dates and numbers follow your region. Your goal titles and notes are never translated.

How do I reduce animations?

In iPhone Settings, choose Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion.

如何让花开花？

种下目标，设定总量和单位。每次记录实际进度，植物会逐渐成长。累计达到目标后，花朵自动收藏到「我的花园」，无需等待真实天数。

中断后能继续吗？

可以。进度只累积，不因缺席而减少。

如何改名或删除？

点开目标详情，在右上角管理菜单中修改名称或删除。删除目标会一并删除该目标的全部记录与收藏，无法撤销。误记的进度可以在成长记录中撤销。

数据保存在哪里？

目标和记录保存在设备本地。没有账号或独立云同步。卸载应用可能造成数据丢失；系统备份取决于你的设备设置。

如何减弱动画？

在 iPhone 设置 → 辅助功能 → 动态效果中开启「减弱动态效果」。
